About Me

Questions?

FAQ

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes about 3–5 days. A business website usually takes 7–10 days, and an online store 2–3 weeks, depending on how quickly content is ready.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting?

Yes, you'll need a domain (like yourbusiness.in), which costs roughly ₹500–₹1,000 per year. I'll help you buy it and set up fast, reliable hosting, often free for small sites.

What do you need from me?

Your logo (if you have one), photos, business details and a short description of your services. Don't have content? I can help write it.

How do payments work?

50% advance to start, and the remaining 50% after you approve the final website and before it goes live.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes. I can set up an easy editor for you, or you can choose the Care Plan and I'll handle updates for you.
